Our Story

Small batch, slow and steady

Surga Bakery started as a stall at a Sunday market in Melbourne's inner north before it found a proper home behind its own front door. The way we bake hasn't changed much since. We mill and mix in small batches, shape every loaf by hand, and let a slow fermentation do most of the work. It takes longer, but it's the difference between bread that fills you up and bread that actually tastes like something.

We work with Australian growers for our flour and keep the ingredient list short on purpose. If it wouldn't be in your own kitchen, it's not in our dough.
